Curriculum Vitae

I am a Professor of Economics at the University of Oxford.

I coordinate the Machine Learning and Economics Group at Oxford.

My research interests include:
  • Machine learning theory; the social impact of algorithmic decision making; the political economy of AI
  • Economic inequality; basic income and job guarantee programs
  • Publication bias, pre-analysis plans, and statistics as a social process
  • Adaptive experimental design
  • Statistical decision theory; Empirical Bayes methods
  • Identification and causality
